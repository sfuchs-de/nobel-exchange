Privacy, plainly

Economics 2026 · Updated October 8, 2026

This is a friendly game using imaginary credits. There is no real money, purchase, cash prize, advertising or analytics.

What sign-in does

Google verifies your identity so each account can have one portfolio. We request identity only—not access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or other private Google files. Google’s sign-in service receives the normal browser information needed for authentication.

The server verifies the Google sign-in token and issues an eight-hour app session. That app token stays in this browser tab’s session storage; it is not included in public data. Google’s own browser cookies remain Google’s responsibility.

What is stored and shared

Cloudflare stores your Google account identifier, a random public entry ID, chosen display name, saved allocations, versions, timestamps and retry receipts. The Google sign-in token is not written to the game database.

Before closing, only aggregate backing and supporter counts are public. At closing, chosen display names and final portfolios become public. Results and final scores are public after settlement. Your email, Google account identifier and sign-in token are never part of the public feed.

GitHub Pages hosts the interface and public candidate facts, not participant records. The app has no connection to private household, financial or research systems. Public fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts.

Support and removal

Contact sfuchs.de@gmail.com privately for support or a data-removal request. Do not post private account details in public GitHub issues.

Account-linked data will be removed manually by January 10, 2027. Public nicknames, final picks and scores may remain in the edition archive. Cloudflare’s recovery window can temporarily retain earlier versions. This is a manual retention commitment, not an automatic purge already running.
